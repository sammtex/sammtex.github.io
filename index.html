01 — About

Independent, hands-on publishing.

Said Baha writes and designs books for a niche readership rather than a mass market — practical guides for people building their own income online, and quiet, screen-free activity books for readers who want to slow down.

Every title is planned, written, laid out, and published independently, from first outline to a finished, print-ready interior and cover. New titles are added to the catalog on an ongoing basis.
